## Handling

Verify the reference files from the repo root using `sha256sum -c evidence/SHA256SUMS`. Treat E-01 and E-02 as source artifacts. Save exports separately and document the source capture, frame or stream, acquisition method, analyst name, and hash. Record the capture timestamp or relative offset used in the report. A matching hash verifies byte equality with the known lab artifact.

A replay capture and its exported object are separate evidence items. Review your replay files and screenshots before publication and keep any unrelated traffic or personal information out of the public repo.
